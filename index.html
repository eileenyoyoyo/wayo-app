Our philosophy

Every piece begins with a behaviour, not a trend.

We study how animals move, rest and feel safe — then design objects that answer those instincts with the restraint of fine furniture.

Hide

Enclosed, low-light retreats that let pets observe without being seen — the original peekaboo.

Perch

Elevated vantage points for surveying their kingdom — sculptural towers and window shelves.

Scratch

Natural sisal, cork and wool surfaces for marking, stretching and claw care — beautifully.

Nest

Bolstered, round forms that support the circling and curling instinct for deep, secure sleep.

Craft & material

Made to be lived on, not just looked at.

Each PEEKABOO piece is built by a small family workshop from solid walnut, white oak and ash, joined by hand and finished with plant-based oils that are safe for curious tongues.

Our textiles — bouclé, felted New Zealand wool, natural sisal — are chosen for how they feel under paw and how they age in your home. Every cushion is removable and machine-washable; every scratch surface can be replaced, so the frame lasts a lifetime.
